    Wait — why is ZUS's menu on a marketing agency's site?

    Fair question. We ran a full SEO audit of zuscoffee.com, found their menu page doesn't exist, and figured the funniest way to prove a point was to just… rank for it ourselves. Here's what we found.

    /menu → /rewards
    No menu page

    ZUS has no menu URL and no Menu link in their nav, so Google gives "ZUS coffee menu" to foodpanda, listicles and their own forgotten staging server — not zuscoffee.com.

    ~880 pages
    Canonical leak

    Around 880 of their pages tell Google their "real" version lives on a private staging site they built before launch and seemingly forgot. Google believed them.

    19.5s
    Time to first byte

    Their homepage takes ~19.5s to start loading and weighs 34 MB. Starbucks Malaysia: ~40 ms. This page is a few KB.

    96%
    Brand-only traffic

    96% of ZUS's search traffic is people who already typed "zus." Almost no one discovers them — a hard ceiling for a brand expanding regionally.
